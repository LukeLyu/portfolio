About me

My passion for UX began in 2008 during my undergraduate studies at the University of Manchester, which inspired me to pursue a Master's degree in Human-Computer Interaction at the University of Bath. Since then, I have spent more than 10 years designing user-centered digital products across music streaming, fintech, education, e-commerce, and enterprise platforms.

Currently, I am a Lead UX Designer at NetEase Cloud Music, leading the podcast experience from user research and interaction design to usability testing and mentoring junior designers. My work has contributed to significant user growth and engagement through data-driven, end-to-end design.

Having worked in both in-house product teams and design agencies, I enjoy solving complex problems while balancing user needs with business goals. Beyond product design, I also deliver UX training, write design articles, and actively share knowledge with the design community. Outside work, I enjoy traveling, scuba diving, dancing, reading, and football, all of which continually inspire my creativity and empathy as a designer.

关于我

对用户体验的热爱是从2008年开始的，当时在曼彻斯特大学攻读本科，这段经历激励我在巴斯大学继续深造，获得了人机交互硕士学位。此后，我花了超过十年的时间，为音乐流媒体、金融科技、教育、电商和企业平台设计以用户为中心的数字产品。

目前，我是网易云音乐的高级用户体验设计师，负责播客体验的设计工作，涵盖用户研究、交互设计、可用性测试，以及指导初级设计师。我的设计通过数据驱动帮助产品实现了用户增长和活跃度的明显提升。

我既在产品团队内部工作过，也曾在设计机构任职，善于在满足用户需求与实现商业目标之间找到平衡，解决复杂问题。除了产品设计，我也会给团队做UX培训、写写设计文章，平时也喜欢在社区里跟同行分享交流。工作之外，我喜欢旅行、舞蹈、阅读和足球，这些爱好不断激发我的创造力，也让我做设计的时候更能站在用户角度去感受。
